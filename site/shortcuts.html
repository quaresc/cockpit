In the palette

Enter goes to what it found. Over a Claude session:

ctrl+rTypes its reference at the prompt (a PR's URL, a ticket and its URL, a release and its verdict)
ctrl+aAsks the session about it
ctrl+fForks the session into the actions space to look into it

It also jumps to a pinned space, refreshes the inbox, shows the doctor's report or today's brief, forks the session below to a tab and opens its folder in your editor.

Cmd+K in Ghostty

Make the key type herdr's prefix then a space. With the default ctrl+b, in ~/.config/ghostty/config:

keybind = cmd+k=text:\x02\x20

For another prefix, ctrl+<letter> is the letter's alphabet position in hex (ctrl+a \x01, ctrl+s \x13), F12 is \x1b[24~. This replaces Ghostty's clear screen.
