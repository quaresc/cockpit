Herdr TUI

cockpit, in your terminal

A plugin for herdr: what each agent works on, right beside it. /cockpit:setup or cockpit setup installs it.

Install Keys

What's in it

Home is app-only; the blueprint is an APP shot.

Sidebar

Each agent's task, branch, ticket, CI and page. Red on failing CI.

FooterClaude Code

Under Claude Code's prompt, from the cockpit plugin: one click to the ticket, PR, deploy, preview and every page the conversation cites.

Inbox

Reviews, then your tickets with their PRs, CI and deploys; one key per pinned space.

Show panesNew

Charts, tables and diffs beside the agent, drawn on its own; comment a diff as one review.

APP shot

Blueprint

The plan the agent keeps current, drawn as text in its pane; clicks are app-only.

E2E beside the agent

The agent tests its page in terminal-browser, next to the conversation.

Before and after

Its screenshots side by side, main against the branch, on phone and desktop.

Palette

prefix space over any pane: tickets, PRs, runbooks, meetings, history.

Recap

While you were away: what each agent did, the files it changed, what it waits for.

Runbook

The steps left to ship, refreshed in the background as PRs and deploys move.

To-do

Your list, with the tickets and links it mentions.

Meetings

Your week with join and a prepared brief.

Monitoropt-in

Each release judged on errors and latency, from Datadog.

Routinesopt-in

Agent prompts and scripts the hub runs on a schedule or an event.

Notifications

System notifications whose click leads somewhere, with buttons (macOS, and Linux through notify-send).

Settings

/cockpit settings in a pane: change a value from any session.

Keys

In every app: j k move, Enter main action, / filter.
