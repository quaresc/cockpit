Herdr APP

cockpit, drawn natively

A layout and a mod for the Herdr desktop app: Home, a sidebar of agents, every app as a view. /cockpit:setup installs it, or cockpit app install.

Install Keys

What's in it

  • HomeKPI tiles, the next move with its choices as buttons, agents by stage; Up next, Apps and Activity.
  • SidebarAgents that need you, answered in place; every agent by ticket; quota bars; a bubble per app.
  • AgentsFloating terminal cards with CPU and memory; tabs and spaces named after their work.
  • Side panelShow, Review, Runbook, Files and Changes beside the focused agent; its links in the status bar.
  • Live blueprintThe agent's plan as a diagram it keeps current. Click a box to ask for a change.
  • InboxAlerts, reviews, your PRs, tickets and cleanup; merge, or start an agent in a worktree.
  • Palette⌘⇧P: Herdr's commands plus everything cockpit knows, history and past sessions.
  • Runbook · To-do · MeetingsThe steps left to ship, your list, your week with join and a prepared brief.
  • Monitor opt-inEach release judged on errors and latency, from Datadog.
  • Routines opt-inClaude prompts and scripts on a schedule or an event; each run's output as Markdown.
  • Recap · Queue · ResumeEvery agent recapped by stage, prompts queued for a busy one, agents back after a restart.
  • NotificationsHerdr's toast in front, the system's otherwise; a click opens the row it is about.
