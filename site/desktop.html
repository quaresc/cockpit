Herdr APP

cockpit, drawn natively

A layout and a mod for the Herdr desktop app: Home, a sidebar of agents, every app as a view. /cockpit:setup or cockpit setup installs it.

Install Keys

What's in it

Every view on the acme demo; notifications are a TUI shot.

Home

KPI tiles, the next move with its choices as buttons, agents by stage; Up next, Apps and Activity.

Agents and sidebar

Agents as floating cards with CPU and memory; questions answered in the sidebar; tabs and spaces name themselves.

Side panel and chartsNew

Show, Review, Runbook, Files and Changes beside the agent; it draws figure tiles, area, donut and bar charts unasked.

Live blueprint

The agent's plan as a diagram it keeps current. Click a box to ask for a change.

Editor beside the agent

Read and edit a file next to the agent; its edits show live, ⌘S saves. Ask the agent about any lines.

Diff of its changes

Uncommitted, branch or PR, side by side or unified, a click from the agent.

Comments sent back

Comment line by line, then Submit review sends every note to the agent as one message.

Guided walkthrough

The diff explained in reading order, steps you can follow, untested parts called out.

Inbox

Alerts, reviews, your PRs, tickets and cleanup; merge, or start an agent in a worktree.

Palette

⌘⇧P: Herdr's commands plus everything cockpit knows, history and past sessions.

Every agent · Recap

⌘R: agents by stage with their question and recap; prompts queue for a busy one.

Runbook

The steps left to ship a ticket, each with its PR, deploy or command to run.

To-do

Your list, with the tickets and links it mentions.

Meetings

Your week as a list, day or week, with join and a prepared brief.

Mailopt-in

Unread mail split into what needs you and updates, each thread rendered beside the list.

Monitoropt-in

Each release judged on errors and latency, from Datadog.

Routinesopt-in

Agent prompts and scripts on a schedule or an event; each run's output as Markdown.

TUI shot

Notifications

Herdr's toast in front, the system's otherwise; a click opens the row it is about.

Keys

Every app keeps its TUI keys, plus buttons and right-click menus.
