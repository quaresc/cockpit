Herdr APP

cockpit as a layout and a mod in the Herdr desktop app. /cockpit:setup installs it, or cockpit app install.

Home

The next move, and every agent and PR by whose move it is. Answer, merge or recap from the keyboard.

Sidebar

The agents that need you, answered in place, every agent by ticket, and a bubble on each app.

Inbox

Reviews, then your tickets with their PRs, CI and deploys. A click on a reference opens it.

Runbook · To-do · Meetings

The steps left to ship a ticket, your to-do list, your week with join and prepare.

Monitor · Routines

Each release judged on errors and latency; Claude prompts the hub runs on a schedule. Both opt-in.

Beside each agent

A side panel with its show panes, review, runbook, files and changes; its links in the status bar.

Palette

⌘⇧P: Herdr’s commands plus everything cockpit knows, the week’s history and past sessions.
