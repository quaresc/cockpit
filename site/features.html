Features

cockpit lives in three places: the Herdr desktop app, herdr in the terminal, and Claude Code itself. The screenshots are the real apps on made-up data.

Herdr app

cockpit runs in the Herdr desktop app, drawn natively by Herdr on the same hub and data as the terminal half: both can run at once. Every app has all of its terminal keys and actions, as visible buttons with their key, plus hover cards, right-click menus, drag to reorder, sortable tables and details beside the lists.

Home

Where the day stands, at a glance:

  • Today's numbers: landed today with seven days of bars, needs you, working, ready to land, agent spend today and over seven days.
  • The next move: the longest-waiting question with its choices as buttons and a reply field, else a PR to fix, else one to land, else all clear.
  • The agents and PRs by whose move it is, in four columns: Needs you, Working, Ready to land, Done.
  • Your delivery: this week against the same days last week, two weeks of bars, the median time to merge, open PRs, reviews and spend.

Answer, merge, recap, snooze or start a new agent from the keyboard; right-click for the rest.

Sidebar

In place of Herdr's spaces and agents: the agents that need you, with their question answered in place (single choice, several, or free text), every agent by ticket with its PR, CI and changes, Claude quota bars with a pace tick, the spaces, and the apps with their counts.

Inbox

Review requests, then your tickets by project with their stacked PRs, CI, deploys and previews, with details beside the list.

  • Merge with the PR's readiness spelled out, auto-merge, ready and draft.
  • Start a ticket or a PR in a worktree with an agent, after checks: open, not someone else's, no space on it yet. A ticket also goes In Progress, assigned to you.
  • The ticket's Linear status and assignee, PR alerts by diff, uncommitted work and merged worktrees to clean.

Runbook

The steps left to ship each ticket, written and kept current by /runbook. The focused agent's runbook also sits in the work view's side panel.

To-do

A Markdown list edited in place, ticket keys and links clickable. /todo <text> adds to it from any session.

Meetings

Your week as a list or day by day, with join and prepare on each meeting.

Monitor opt-in

Production's verdict as a dashboard: every new release, judged once it has run a while on errors, p95 and new Datadog errors before and after, with alerting monitors and each app's history as meters and strips.

From a release or an error: investigate, fix it in a draft PR, file a bug or roll back.

Routines opt-in

Claude prompts and scripts the hub runs on its own, on a schedule or when something happens, as a dashboard: each run's answer, cost and session, with run, stop, pause, edit, and a talk over a run. See routines below for what a routine is.

Agents

  • Work: the tabs, or the focused agent alone (⌘⌥A), with a side panel (⌘⌥B): Show, Review, the ticket's runbook, Files, Changes.
  • Recap (⌘R) of every agent by whose move it is. An agent's recap is asked of haiku a minute after its turn ends, when nobody looked at it.
  • Prompt queue per agent (⌘⌥Q): line up prompts, reorder them, send one or all.
  • Bell: the agents waiting; a click goes to the longest waiting, hovering shows their questions.
  • Status bar: Home's counts, the focused agent's branch, files, CI and PR, quota bars, queued prompts and agents to resume.
  • Resume after a restart (⌘⌥R): the Claude sessions a reboot stopped come back in their terminals.

Show panes

A pane beside the agent, or the Show tab of the side panel following the focused agent: charts, tables, flowcharts and sequence diagrams drawn natively, pictures, before/after compares, live Metabase or Datadog data, logs, questions, and diffs you comment line by line.

Review and walkthrough

The focused agent's changes, uncommitted, on the branch or in the PR, as a diff (folding files, unified or side by side). Comment line by line and send the comments back to the agent as one message, or read the change as a guided walkthrough: steps whose code moved show as Outdated, new hunks land in Not covered.

Palette

⌘⇧P, Herdr's own plus cockpit's: every command with its key, layouts, spaces, tabs and agents, the doctor's report, today's brief, and the hub's search.

Over a Claude agent, put what you found in its prompt, ask it, fork it, or open its folder in your editor (Remote-SSH for a remote space).

Notifications

Herdr's toast while the window is in front, the system's otherwise. A click opens the row it is about (a PR or ticket in the inbox, a runbook, a routine), where its buttons are.

Beside each agent

🧭 Sidebar

Each agent's task, branch, ticket, CI and page, plus counts on the pinned spaces. Turns red on failing CI or when two agents share a branch.

🔗 Footer

One click to the ticket, PR, deploy dashboard, preview and every page the conversation cites.

🧪 E2E tests

The agent opens its page in terminal-browser beside it, phone then desktop, main against the branch, and puts the before/after in the PR. Set e2eBrowser to chrome to use Claude in Chrome instead.

📊 Show

A pane beside the agent for charts, tables, diagrams, pictures, before/after compares, live Metabase or Datadog data, questions, and diffs you comment line by line.

🔔 Notifications

Each with buttons that act: Review, Why?, Rerun, Plan, Join.

☀️ Briefs

A morning brief, meeting briefs, a recap when you come back to a space, a check after each deploy.

Pinned spaces

📥 Inbox

Review requests, then your tickets by project with their stacked PRs, CI, deploys, previews and Claude usage cost.

  • Every row has its actions; pick several rows to act on them at once. / filters.
  • Review threads and failing CI show beside a PR.
  • A row flashes when its CI turns or an agent finishes, and is marked when it changed since your last visit.
  • Reviews, plans and talks about a ticket (forked from the session that worked on it) open in the actions space.

📋 Runbook

/runbook writes the steps left to ship a ticket and keeps them current. Steps light up as they get done; filter, edit and add them in place.

📝 To-do

A Markdown list you edit in place, ticket keys and links clickable. /todo <text> adds to it from any session.

📅 Meetings

Your week as a list or day by day, with join and prepare on each meeting.

🩺 Monitor opt-in

Add monitor to spaces. Every new release of the environment, judged once it has run a while: errors, p95 and new Datadog errors before and after, alerting monitors, a day of numbers per app. From a release or an error, investigate, fix it in a draft PR, file a bug or roll back.

⏰ Routines opt-in

Add routines to spaces. Claude prompts and scripts the hub runs on its own: on a schedule (weekdays 09:00, every 30m, cron), or when something happens (on ci-failed, on review, on release-moved).

Each run's answer, estimated cost and session stay with it, each routine picks its model (lightest loads nothing but the tools it calls), and a weekly budget can cap a routine. Run, stop, pause, edit or talk a run over from the space. a adds one, /routine <when and what> asks Claude to write it. Local: they run while the hub does.

⚡ Actions space

A tab per review, plan or talk started from the inbox or the runbook, instead of new spaces. Pressing an action again while it runs brings its tab back.

📌 Your own spaces

Pin a notes repo or a scratch shell beside the built-in spaces, with its folder, icon and key. See spaces.

Everywhere

🔎 Palette

prefix+space (or Cmd+K in Ghostty) over any pane, ctrl+p in every app. Finds tickets, PRs, runbook steps, meetings, releases, the week's history and past sessions, by what was said or what they were about ("the session where we fixed quotas").

Over a Claude session, put what you found in its prompt, ask it about it or fork it; fork the session itself to a tab, or open its folder in your editor. Claude searches the same way.

⚙️ Settings

/cockpit flips what you change day to day.

Claude Code plugin

The Claude Code half fetches from your sessions' connectors, one session at a time, and hands the result to the hub, which keeps the data for both halves. Each session also tells the hub what it works on: its repo, branch, ticket, task, the page beside it, context, cost, PR and CI. That is what the sidebar, Home and the footer show.

Commands

CommandDoes
/cockpit:setupPicks the features, writes ~/.config/cockpit/config.json, wires herdr and the Herdr app.
/cockpit:doctorChecks every part of cockpit and says how to fix what is broken.
/cockpit [setting value]The settings pane; /cockpit e2e chrome and the like set one directly.
/runbook [ticket]Where the work stands (PRs, deploys, prod commands) as a table, also shown in the runbook space.
/routine <when and what>Writes a routine the hub runs on its own, on a schedule or when something happens.
/todo [text]Adds to the to-do list; alone, lists it.

Tools Claude gets

show

Charts, tables, diagrams, pictures, diffs, logs or a question in a pane beside the session, live from Metabase or Datadog when asked.

cockpit_search · cockpit_changes

Claude searches what the palette searches, and reads the week's history: reviews, CI, tickets, merges, deploys, releases.

report_step

A session handed a runbook step records how it went, and the step's status follows.

side-browser skill

The browser pane beside the conversation, for E2E tests on phone, tablet and desktop sizes; falls back to Claude in Chrome.

Briefs and checks

A morning brief, a brief before each meeting (reading the docs it links), a recap when you come back to a space, and a check after each deploy. Each runs when the config says and with the tools it allows.
